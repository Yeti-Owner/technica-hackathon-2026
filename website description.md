# Our Mission
We want to help people with accessibility issues and make sure everyone has an equal chance to use the internet.

# What It Does
We built this app for people who have a hard time with fine motor skills. It completely removes the need to type or struggle with a standard mouse. Instead, you can easily click on exactly what you want, select buttons, and scroll up and down web pages entirely hands-free. It handles the difficult physical movements for you, letting you browse the internet comfortably without the frustration that usually comes with typing and clicking.

# How It Helps
For people who have trouble using their hands or fingers due to conditions like arthritis, cerebral palsy, ALS, or spinal cord injuries, just using a computer can be exhausting or even impossible. This app gives people their independence back. It allows users to read the news, pay bills, shop, and chat with friends all on their own. By trading the fine hand movements of a normal mouse for simple head movements and voice commands, it takes away the physical strain of getting online.

# Why We're Doing This
Being able to use the internet shouldn't be a luxury. Right now, the special hardware needed to help people use computers can cost thousands of dollars, putting a huge financial strain on families. We are building this app to fix that problem. We believe a physical disability shouldn't keep anyone from being part of the digital world. By making this a software you can run on the basic computer parts you already own, we want to provide an affordable way for anyone to get online easily.
