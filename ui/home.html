What Nod does

A computer you control without your hands

No typing required

Nod completely removes the need to type or struggle with a standard mouse. Click on exactly what you want, select buttons, and scroll up and down web pages — entirely hands-free.

Built for independence

For people who have trouble using their hands due to conditions like arthritis, cerebral palsy, ALS, or spinal cord injuries, just using a computer can be exhausting. Nod gives people their independence back.

Simple head movements

By trading the fine hand movements of a normal mouse for simple head movements and voice commands, Nod takes away the physical strain of getting online.

Affordable access

Special accessibility hardware can cost thousands of dollars. Nod is software that runs on the basic computer parts you already own — no expensive equipment needed.

Our mission

Being able to use the internet shouldn't be a luxury.

We want to help people with accessibility issues and make sure everyone has an equal chance to use the internet. A physical disability shouldn't keep anyone from being part of the digital world. Read the news, pay bills, shop, chat with friends — all on your own, comfortably, without frustration.
