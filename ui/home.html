Our Mission

Being able to use the internet shouldn't be a luxury.

We want to make sure everyone has an equal chance to explore the digital world. A physical disability or motor limitation should never keep anyone from reading the news, paying bills, shopping, or chatting with friends — comfortably, independently, and without fatigue.

Why Nod Matters

A computer you control without your hands

01

No typing required

Nod completely removes the need to type or struggle with a standard mouse. Click on exactly what you want, select buttons, and scroll up and down web pages — entirely hands-free.

Effortless interactions
02

Simple head movements

By trading the fine hand movements of a normal mouse for simple head movements and voice commands, Nod takes away the physical strain of getting online.

Zero wrist or hand fatigue
03

Built for independence

For people with arthritis, cerebral palsy, ALS, or spinal cord injuries, just using a computer can be exhausting. Nod restores independence so anyone can explore online freely.

Empowering autonomy
04

Affordable access

Special accessibility hardware can cost thousands of dollars. Nod is lightweight software that runs on standard computers with standard webcams — no expensive gear required.

Available to anyone
